# Privacy

Serenity Blocks is made by Olov Melander, Sweden. Contact: [contact address to be added before release]

The game has no servers of its own and no analytics, advertising or tracking. Nothing you do in the
game is sent to the developer. What the game keeps, it keeps on your computer; what it shares, it
shares through Steam, for the features you use.

## On your computer

The game keeps these in its data folder:

- your settings, progress, records and play history;
- replays of your single-player games, which hold no names;
- your Steam name and ID, a copy of your Steam statistics and anything still waiting to be sent
  to Steam, so that the game works while Steam is offline;
- your breathing practice log: when you practised, for how long, how long each hold was and the
  intention you picked;
- the players you have muted in online chat, with their Steam name and ID, and that you have read
  the notice shown before online play;
- a log of the game's connection to Steam, written afresh each time the game starts: your Steam
  name and ID, the folder the game is installed in, and the Steam IDs of players in your lobby;
- a crash report, if the game crashes. It is a snapshot of the game's memory, so it can contain
  names or chat. It stays in the folder and is only ever sent anywhere if you send it yourself,
  for example when asking for help.

The folder is `%APPDATA%\serenity-blocks` on Windows and `~/.config/serenity-blocks` on Linux.
To delete all of it, close the game and delete that folder; uninstalling the game does not remove
it. Steam Cloud brings your saves back the next time you play, unless Steam Cloud is off for the
game or its Cloud files are removed too.

## Through Steam

When you play through Steam, these features use your Steam account. Valve runs Steam, in the USA,
and the [Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/) covers what
Valve stores and for how long. To see whether Steam can be reached, the game may also open a
connection to Steam's servers itself; it sends nothing over it.

- **Steam Cloud** keeps a copy of your saves with your Steam account, so they follow you to
  another computer: unlocked themes, Odyssey progress, records, lifetime statistics, key and
  controller bindings and your sound, theme, effect and control settings, with a random ID for
  each computer so that the copies can be merged. The files are `cloud_manifest.json`,
  `unlocks.json`, `odyssey.json`, `settings.json`, `keybinds.json`, `highscores.json` and
  `stats.json`. You can turn Steam Cloud off in Steam.
- **Statistics**: when a game ends, the game adds to your Steam statistics: games played, lines
  cleared, minutes played, your longest Infinity run and best cascade, your Odyssey stars, and
  your online matches, wins and frags.
- **Your status**: your Steam friends can see that you are playing, and what: the mode, a level
  or chapter where there is one, and how many players are in your online lobby or match. In a
  match open to anyone or to friends, they can also join you from there.
- **Online play** uses Steam lobbies. A lobby holds the match name you type, its rules and how
  many players are in it; when the match is open to anyone, everyone browsing the match list sees
  them. Players in your lobby see your Steam ID and your name, as your game reports it.
- **Connections**: from the moment you join a lobby, also when you only watch, your computer is
  connected to the match's host, peer to peer and directly when Steam can; the host is connected
  to every player and everyone watching. If the host drops out during a match, one of the players
  takes over and the others connect to that player. The players you are connected to can see your
  IP address, as in most online games. The game connects only to players in the lobby you are in.
- **Chat**: messages you type in an online lobby, match or results screen go to the players in
  that lobby, passed on by the host. Each player's game keeps them only while the session lasts
  and never saves them. In a match open to anyone, you see no chat and can send none unless you
  turn on "Chat in public matches" in Settings › General. There you can also turn all chat off,
  and see and change who you have muted: a muted player's messages are hidden from you, they
  aren't told, and the list stays on your computer. "View Steam profile", beside Mute, opens a
  player's profile in Steam, where Steam's own tools to block or report a player are.

If you play without Steam, none of this happens.

## The web version

The free web version is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web host, GitHub sees the IP address of
a visitor's browser; GitHub's own privacy statement covers that. The game keeps your settings and
saves in the browser's storage for the site, which you can clear in the browser. It sets no
cookies and has no online play.

## Why, who and how long

- **Why**: the game uses this data only to run the features you choose to use: keeping your
  saves, Steam Cloud, your status, online matches and chat. That is also its legal ground: those
  features cannot work without it.
- **Who receives it**: Valve, in the USA, under the Steam Privacy Policy; the other players in
  your lobby; and GitHub, for the web version. The developer receives none of it.
- **How long**: what is on your computer stays until you delete it. The Steam connection log is
  replaced at each start, and chat is gone when the session ends. What Steam holds stays with
  your Steam account, as the Steam Privacy Policy says.

## Your rights

You can ask to see the data about you, to have it corrected or deleted, and you can object to its
use. The developer holds none of it: what is on your computer you can read, change or delete
yourself, as described above, and for what Valve holds, the Steam Privacy Policy says how. For
anything else, or for help with either, write to the contact address above. If you do, the
developer gets your message and the address it came from, and uses them to answer you.

You can also complain to the Swedish Authority for Privacy Protection (IMY, imy.se) or to the
data protection authority in your own country.

## Children

The game is not directed at children. Online play and chat work only with a Steam account.
