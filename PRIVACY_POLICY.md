# Privacy

Serenity Blocks is made by Olov Melander. Contact: [contact address to be added before release]

The game has no servers of its own and no analytics, advertising or tracking. Nothing you do in the
game is sent to the developer.

## On your computer

The game keeps your settings, progress, records, breathing practice and saved replays on your
computer, in the game's data folder. If the game crashes, a crash report is written to the same
folder and stays there; it is only ever sent anywhere if you send it yourself, for example when
asking for help. A short log of the game's connection to Steam is kept there too.

You can delete all of it by deleting the game's data folder.

## Through Steam

When you play through Steam, these features use your Steam account. Valve runs them, and the
[Steam Privacy Policy](https://store.steampowered.com/privacy_agreement/) covers what Valve stores.

- **Steam Cloud** keeps a copy of your save data with your Steam account, so it follows you to
  another computer. You can turn it off in Steam.
- **Leaderboards and achievements**: your scores, times and achievements are stored by Steam and
  shown with your Steam name to other players.
- **Your status**: your Steam friends can see that you are playing, and what: the mode, and a
  level or chapter where there is one.
- **Online play** uses Steam lobbies. Players in the same lobby see your Steam name and avatar.
  During a match the game connects your computer to theirs (peer to peer), directly when it can,
  so players in your match can see your IP address, as in most online games. The game only
  connects to players in the lobby you are in.

If you play without Steam, none of this happens.

## Questions

Ask at the contact address above. The developer holds no personal data about you; for data Valve
holds about your Steam account, see the Steam Privacy Policy.
