# 🕉️ Meditation Temple Theme - Complete Redesign

**Base Inspiration:** Buddha silhouette at sunrise/sunset with temple stupas and layered clouds
**Theme Name:** Meditation Temple

## Mood & Style

- **Atmosphere:** Serene, spiritual, contemplative, peaceful awakening at golden hour
- **Color Palette:**
  - Main: Warm oranges (#E67E22, #F39C12), golden yellows (#F9E79F, #F7DC6F), soft amber (#F8B500)
  - Accents: Deep burgundy/brown silhouettes (#5D4E37, #3E2723), soft white clouds (#FFF5E1, #FAEBD7)
  - Sky gradient: Deep orange → golden yellow → cream
- **Emotions:** Peace, mindfulness, spiritual awakening, tranquility, inner stillness

---

## 🌌 Layer Breakdown

### Layer 1 – Sky / Background
- **Radial gradient** from center (cream/pale yellow) to edges (deep orange/amber)
- Creates the glowing sun effect behind the Buddha silhouette
- Subtle animated glow pulse to simulate sunrise/sunset breathing

### Layer 2 – Large Radiant Sun
- **Large circular sun** positioned at the Buddha's head level (center-background)
- Soft glow/halo effect around the edges
- Pale yellow center (#FEF5E7) fading to golden edges
- Optional: Very subtle scale pulse (0.98 to 1.02) for living quality

### Layer 3 – Cloud Layers (Background)
- **Soft white/cream clouds** positioned behind the temple silhouettes
- Layered horizontal bands suggesting depth and distance
- Very slow horizontal drift (opposite directions for parallax)
- Semi-transparent (opacity 0.7-0.9)

### Layer 4 – Temple Stupas (Far Background)
- **Distant temple stupas** on the horizon line (smaller, faded)
- Dark burgundy/brown silhouettes (#5D4E37) with reduced opacity (0.4-0.5)
- Iconic Buddhist stupa shapes with pointed spires
- Multiple stupas at varying heights creating skyline
- Slowest parallax movement

### Layer 5 – Temple Stupas (Mid-distance)
- **Mid-distance temple structures** slightly larger and more defined
- Darker silhouettes (#3E2723) with higher opacity (0.6-0.7)
- More detail visible in architecture
- Medium parallax speed

### Layer 6 – Buddha Silhouette (Center Focus)
- **Large Buddha silhouette** in meditation pose (seated, hands in mudra)
- Positioned center screen, slightly lower than vertical center
- Deep brown/black (#1A1A1A, #2C1810) completely opaque
- Backlit by the sun creating iconic rim-light effect
- Static (no movement) - anchor point for the scene
- Subtle inner glow/aura around edges (optional)

### Layer 7 – Temple Stupas (Foreground)
- **Nearest temple silhouettes** on left and right edges
- Largest, darkest, sharpest (#1A1A1A)
- Frame the Buddha composition
- Fastest (but still slow) parallax movement

### Layer 8 – Atmospheric Overlay & Particles
- **Golden light particles** (dust motes, incense smoke, spiritual energy)
- Floating gently upward and across the scene
- Very small, soft glowing dots (#F8B500, #FDE184)
- Creates magical, peaceful atmosphere
- Optional: Subtle warm fog/haze at bottom (amber tinted)

---

## ✨ Optional Dynamic Notes

- **Sun Breathing:** The sun can have a very subtle scale pulse (3-5 second cycles) to give life
- **Parallax Layers:**
  - Layer 3 (clouds): 240s drift
  - Layer 4 (far stupas): 200s scroll
  - Layer 5 (mid stupas): 150s scroll
  - Layer 7 (near stupas): 100s scroll
- **Special Feature:** The Buddha silhouette acts as the focal anchor - all other elements move around it, creating a meditative stability in the center of a living world
- **Light Particles:** Rise from bottom to top with gentle horizontal drift,循环 spawning for continuous effect
- **Color Warmth:** Can subtly shift between dawn (cooler oranges) and dusk (warmer reds) on a long 300s+ cycle
