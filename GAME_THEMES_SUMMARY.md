# 🎮 Serenity Blocks - Complete Theme Guide

A comprehensive summary of all **55 visual themes** available in Serenity Blocks. Each theme creates a unique atmospheric backdrop that transforms your gameplay experience.

---

## 🌍 Biomes (23 themes)

### Forest
A serene forest scene with gentle green ambiance where firefly particles drift through the air, creating a magical woodland atmosphere with soft, natural lighting.

### Himalayan Peak
Majestic mountain peaks reaching toward the sky, capturing the crisp, high-altitude atmosphere of the Himalayas with a sense of grandeur, tranquility, and vastness.

### Ice Temple
A mystical frozen sanctuary featuring crystalline structures and ethereal ice formations. The scene glows with cool blue and white tones, evoking ancient, frozen beauty.

### Moonlit Forest
A forest bathed in soft moonlight where shadows dance and the world takes on a mystical silver-blue hue. The moon casts gentle light through trees, creating a peaceful nocturnal atmosphere.

### Wolfhour
The mysterious twilight hour when the world is caught between day and night. The atmosphere is moody and atmospheric, perfect for contemplative gameplay.

### Ocean
An underwater scene with kelp strands swaying in the current and god rays filtering down from the surface. Marine life and water effects create a sense of depth and movement.

### Sunset
A warm, golden sunset with a dynamic day-night cycle. Rich oranges, pinks, and purples paint the sky while celestial bodies (sun and moon) respond to gameplay events with flares and shockwaves.

### Mountain
Towering peaks and valleys that create a sense of scale and majesty. The mountain landscape offers dramatic vistas and natural beauty.

### Winter
A snowy winter wonderland with gently falling snowflakes. Captures the quiet beauty of winter with soft white landscapes and a crisp, cold atmosphere.

### Fall
Autumn colors dominate with rich reds, oranges, and yellows. Falling leaves drift through the air, capturing the essence of the changing seasons.

### Summer
Bright, warm summer days with vibrant colors and sunny skies. The atmosphere is cheerful and energetic, evoking long summer afternoons.

### Tornado
Swirling wind forms with streaked glow and storm-lit atmosphere. The palette is warm and dramatic, emphasizing motion and energy.

### Sakura Twilight
A beautiful sunset scene with cherry blossom trees. Pink petals fall continuously while glowing paper lanterns and flying bird silhouettes add to the magical twilight atmosphere.

### Koi Pond
A serene top-down view of a koi pond with gracefully swimming fish, bobbing lily pads, water ripples, and cherry blossom petals floating on shimmering water.

### Meadow
An idyllic sun-dappled meadow featuring a warm golden sun with animated rays and lens flare. Drifting clouds create parallax depth, swaying grass responds to wind, colorful wildflowers bloom, butterflies dance, and fireflies glow at dusk.

### Swedish Forest
A Nordic forest scene inspired by John Bauer's artwork, capturing the characteristic beauty of Swedish woodlands with mystical spirits and magical atmosphere.

### Misty Lake
A tranquil lake scene shrouded in mist with aurora borealis dancing overhead. Calm and mysterious with soft fog creating depth, reeds swaying, and lily pads floating.

### Waves
Ocean waves crash and flow in a dynamic, rhythmic scene. The movement of water is the central focus, bringing energy and motion to the background.

### Luminous Tides
Glowing, bioluminescent tides wash ashore, creating an otherworldly beach scene. The water itself seems to glow with inner light.

### Stillwater
A calm, still body of water reflecting the sky above with mystical spirits and trolls. Perfect reflections create symmetry and tranquility.

### Moonlit Greenhouse
A greenhouse bathed in moonlight where plants grow under silver glow. Combines the beauty of nature with glass and metal structure.

### Moonrise Summit
A mountain peak at the moment of moonrise, capturing the transition from day to night with the moon ascending over the summit.

### Verdant Hills *(NEW)*
A peaceful Elysium-inspired 3D rolling hills landscape with fluffy procedural grass swaying in the wind, stylized trees with fluffy foliage, atmospheric gradient fog, and floating particles. Grass responds dynamically to gameplay events with wind gusts.

### Shifting Sands *(NEW)*
An Arrakis-inspired harsh desert planet with twin moons, procedural dunes, volumetric sand smoke, spice particles, and heat shimmer effects. Inspired by DUNE, featuring the "blue-within-blue" glow effect on game events.

## 🌌 Cosmic & Sky (17 themes)

### Aurora
Dynamic northern lights that grow, shrink, fade, and intensify organically. Can appear as a subtle horizon glow, medium curtain, or massive full-screen storm with deep green and emerald colors.

### Galaxy
A multi-layered cosmic experience with nebula clouds that have depth and rotation. Features a dynamic starfield, cosmic dust particles, distant spiral galaxies, supernova bursts, and cosmic ripple waves.

### Geode
A luminous fiber-optic starfield creating an immersive cosmic cavern. Dense stars span the screen with fiber-optic strands floating throughout. Warm cosmic palette featuring oranges, reds, and magentas.

### Aether Tides
Ethereal cosmic tides flowing through space, creating waves of energy and light with a mystical, flowing quality.

### Cosmic Chimes
A celestial scene where cosmic forces create harmonious, chiming effects. Both cosmic and musical, with visual elements suggesting sound and vibration.

### Starlight
A star-filled sky where individual stars twinkle and shine. Captures the beauty of a clear night sky with stars of varying brightness.

### Bioluminescence
An underwater scene filled with glowing bioluminescent plants and crystal formations. Various glowing organisms create their own light in a deep-sea wonderland.

### Crystal Cave
A cave filled with glowing crystals that create their own light. Combines underground mystery with the beauty of crystalline formations.

### Lunara
A breathtaking mystical winter landscape with twinkling colored stars, dynamic multi-layer aurora, twin planets (purple and pink), layered mountain silhouettes, snow-covered pine trees with gentle sway, falling snowflakes, and mystical fog layers.

### Solar Eclipse
The dramatic moment of a solar eclipse where the moon passes before the sun. Captures the unique lighting and atmosphere of this celestial event.

### Black Hole
A gravitational anomaly in space with stars and light being pulled toward a central point. Creates a sense of cosmic power and space-time distortion.

### Supernova
A star exploding in a brilliant burst of light and energy, capturing the moment of stellar death and rebirth with expanding waves of light and color.

### Cosmic Noir
A dark, moody cosmic scene with film noir aesthetics. The palette is darker, creating a mysterious space atmosphere with dramatic lighting.

### Blood Moon
A lunar eclipse where the moon takes on a deep red hue. Dramatic and atmospheric with the red moon casting an eerie glow.

### Astral Weave
Cosmic threads and energies weave together, creating patterns of light suggesting the interconnected nature of the universe.

### Stellar Velocity
Stars streak through space at high velocity, creating motion blur and a sense of incredible speed—like traveling through space at light speed.

### Stellar Drift *(NEW)*
An Andromeda-inspired 3D space theme with a Mars-like gas giant planet featuring flowing bands and storm spots, 3D orbiting particles in a thick disk, vibrant nebula clouds at screen edges, thousands of twinkling stars, and a meteor field.

## 🎨 Abstract & Artistic (6 themes)

### Fluid Dreams
A flowing, dreamlike scene with organic, fluid-like movements. Abstract and surreal, suggesting the fluid nature of dreams and imagination.

### Nebula Flow
A GPU-accelerated fluid dynamics simulation with organic, flowing visual effects. Multiple color schemes (cosmic, ocean, aurora, fire, prismatic) allow for different moods. Reacts spectacularly to gameplay events.

### Electric Dreams
An electric, neon-drenched scene with vibrant colors and energy. Futuristic and energetic with electric currents and neon glows.

### Chromatic Impasto
An artistic scene inspired by thick, textured paint application. Colors are rich and layered, creating depth through visual texture.

### Voltage Storm
A high-energy scene with electric visuals and neon colors. Lightning and shockwave effects create a sense of electrical power and intensity.

### Chromadelic Highway
A psychedelic highway scene with vibrant, shifting colors, suggesting movement and travel through a colorful, abstract landscape.

## 🏙️ Urban & Modern (4 themes)

### Rainy Window
A window during a rainstorm with raindrops running down the glass, ripples, splashes, occasional lightning, and mist particles. Creates a cozy indoor feeling while watching the storm outside.

### Neon Dusk
An urban scene at dusk with neon lights beginning to glow. Captures the transition from day to night in a city with electric colors starting to dominate.

### Synthwave Sunset
A retro-futuristic sunset scene with a grid pattern overlay. The sun moves with animated rays in a distinctive 1980s synthwave aesthetic with vibrant pinks, purples, and oranges.

### Neon District *(NEW)*
A 3D cyberpunk Blade Runner-style immersive street-level view surrounded by towering neon-lit megastructures. Features procedural buildings with glowing windows and Kanji neon signage, rain particle system, atmospheric purple fog, flying vehicles, reflective wet streets, and heavy bloom post-processing.
