# Gravity-first bot rebuild plan

18 November 2026 · Awaiting owner approval for implementation

**Give the live planner credit for what a legal trigger would release, then spend its limited search time on those builds.** The offline player did this with the current piece and three previews. It raised four-wave openings from 2/40 to 20/40 on the audit bags and from 1/40 to 13/40 on fresh bags. Widening the shipped beam alone reached 7/40 and tripled placement work. The production task is to recover a useful share of the first gain inside the frame budget, defend under pressure and distribute that skill across ten playable opponents. ## Changes in order of value

The first row protects the experiment. The following rows are ordered by return, with frame cost checked at every step. The numeric gains below are proposed engineering targets or measured offline comparisons. Each step earns its place through a paired A/B. Gains from different steps overlap.

| Order | Change and reason | Expected gain and keep criterion |
|---|---|---|
| 0 | **Freeze the measuring tool and record the true rewards.** Give the existing benchmark an executable baseline at the audit revision, including inherited evaluator, planner, action order, RNG, cadence and observation behavior. Use the live per-wave score, attack, settled bodies and top-out on both sides of an A/B. Give each live local slot its own seed-derived decision stream independent of the bag and of other slots. | Exact reproduction for the frozen instrument across the 15 campaign seeds and pinned demos. Zero mismatches in score, wave vectors, gross attack and legal-input replay on the parity panel. Today 9/24 opening clears already disagree on score. Full-campaign 9/9 is the historical control to verify before changing a player. |

Use the best real discharge and its trigger pose, wave vector, points and outgoing rows. Treat other shapes as uncertain future capacity, with a separate discounted value. Reward the change in potential across a build and pay the realized reward once on the fire. Retain survival pressure. | First live-budget target of **≥8/40** four-wave openings on the audit panel, at least six above baseline. Target recall ≥80% and precision ≥90% for two-wave visible-shape triggers on a stratified board sample against exhaustive legal landings. **Play surface and goals.** A four-wave fire from empty needs at least 40 cells, or ten tetrominoes. A high tier can build over many spawns while forecasting only a few at a time. Extend a plan when new pieces become public. Give a `combo`/maximum-wave goal credit for the deepest single lock, a cascade-count goal credit for distinct qualifying fires, a Quad goal credit for a four-row wave, and a timed or survival goal credit using its real clock. A rival's objective is winning its own frag race. The human player's bonus-star conditions should never become incentives to sabotage those bonuses. **Work allocation.** Use cheap full-row feasibility checks and shared candidate preparation before expensive trigger enumeration. Keep one ready-to-execute legal route while better routes are explored. Allocate deterministic units across all active local bots per logical game tick. The local fixed-tick loop currently excludes bots. Drive their decisions and input deadlines from the simulation clock and verify replay parity before admitting them to that path. Use wall time for telemetry, while deterministic work counts and visibility govern move choice. Preserve real collision, kick, gravity and per-wave commit semantics. Retain a short, legal defensive plan when the active piece has fallen during planning. A fit or offline ceiling search runs outside the game process. **Tier progression.** Tiers 1–2 should place recognizable pieces and occasionally miss a nearby landing. Tiers 3–4 should see an imminent second wave. Tiers 5–6 should keep a short setup through the next spawn and sometimes give it up to dig. Tiers 7–8 should choose between competing triggers under pressure. Tiers 9–10 should manage a three-preview construction across spawns. Tune the frequency and reliability of these behaviors continuously at the boundaries, then judge them by the paired matches.

## Fitting and comparisons

1. Reserve disjoint bag and decision seeds for training, candidate selection and final evaluation. The published audit seeds, 62001–62040, the campaign gates and D8 panels are now public regression sets. Record a sealed final panel and protocol before fitting. Never select a candidate on its final score or on the campaign gate seeds.
2. Use the private fit harness's background worker pool with a fixed search seed and bounded concurrency at below-normal priority. Start with four workers on this shared machine. Use separate streams for bags, slot decisions, input mistakes and garbage schedules, and feed identical streams to control and candidate. The earlier survival-only fit made every rival stronger. Optimize the mode's real outcomes with survival, human pace and CPU as constraints. Reuse fitted parameters across related tiers, with tier differences chiefly in knowledge, budget, commitment and execution. [Refit record](ODYSSEY_LEVEL_DESIGN_AUDIT_2026-11.md#810-the-bots-weights-re-fitted-2026-11-04)
3. Compare one feature at a time against both the frozen baseline and the last accepted candidate. Save seed-level rows, paired deltas, uncertainty and work counts. Report normal, garbage, near-roof and Infinity cases separately. Check body identity and forecast legality in addition to occupied cells. Only the final candidates consume the sealed panel.
4. After approval, use a separate feature branch and worktree. Deliver the bot and benchmark test results, the paired ledgers, the frame capture, a top-tier construction played through real controls, and a handover naming the chosen rival tiers. Merging requires the separate instruction **“sync with main and merge”**.

## Decisions for approval

1. **Freeze the campaign instrument** at the audited revision. Run the nine gates with that instrument and record candidate live bots in a new series. Recommend approval.
2. **Use legal trigger value as the primary building signal**, permit up to the three public previews, and keep mode-specific score, attack and survival constraints. Recommend the 12/40 audit and 10/40 sealed construction floors.
3. **Enforce an aggregate planning budget before raising search breadth.** Use the proposed 60/165 Hz CPU and reaction-lateness gates. Recommend reducing optional search when the deterministic work allocation is exhausted.
4. **Recalibrate the ten local tiers around a 60–65% adjacent win target**, with the 55–70% acceptance band, plausible seeded mistakes, and a human play review. Recommend approval.
5. **Preserve current paired rival odds and D8's chapter ordering by remapping tiers at existing pace caps.** The larger historical differences for Zephyr and Neon are recorded for a separate level-design decision if desired. Recommend approval of the remap rule without orb changes.
6. **Run the joint fit in the background after approval.** Include search-discovered patterns only if they clear their independent marginal-gain test. Recommend this order.

Implementation starts after approval of this plan.
