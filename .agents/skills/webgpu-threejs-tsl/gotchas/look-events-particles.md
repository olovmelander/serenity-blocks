# Look: gameplay events, particles and bolts

Read before designing how a theme answers locks, clears, combos and level-ups, and before building particles, bursts, bolts or anything aimed "at the board".

**The recurring lessons.** What lands behind the gameplay card is never seen, so events leave the card's edge toward the open stage. Light adds up: budget the TOTAL additive light per tier, give overlapping pools one hue and a capped level, and let reactions accumulate through the shared `GameplaySurge` (`src/themes/shared/gameplay-surge.js`) instead of restarting an envelope per event. Frame-wide flashes go through `flashScale()` (ADR-0021).

One gotcha per line; the number is the row's permanent id (cited elsewhere as "gotcha #NN"). Rows numbered below 300 were numbered before these files existed and keep their numbers, in no order. A NEW row goes at the END of this table and takes the next free number in **1700–1799**. To search: `node .claude/skills/webgpu-threejs-tsl/scripts/gotcha.mjs <words>` (`README.md` in this folder).

| # | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| #144 | A lock's effect "under the piece" is never seen: its ripple, splash or mark always lies behind the gameplay card | The view ray through a piece on the board meets the scene straight behind the card, which is exactly the part of the picture the card covers (and the post's calm zone dims) | Do not aim through the piece. Lay the board over the part of the stage that shows: the column chooses a bearing along the visible arc (left to right as on the board), the row a depth across it (the floor nearest the lens, which is lowest on screen), and something that flies from the piece to that place carries the eye across. Precedent: `pickRingSite` in `src/themes/stellar-drift/stellar-drift-world.js` |
| #188 | An event aimed "beside the board" spawns its particles right in front of the lens (huge bokeh blobs in the sky) once the eye stands on high ground | The camera ray through the screen point hits the ground the eye stands on a few metres away | March the ray from a minimum distance and, while it is still inside the near ground, walk on until it comes out before looking for the hit; try neighbouring screen rows when a ray finds none. `landAt` in `src/themes/painted-ages/painted-ages-world.js` (2026-10-12) |
| #179 | Grounded particles are correctly projected beside the board but disappear, or a settled fan resembles a vertical comb | A projected ground point can sit behind a nearer ridge; a fan spanning a sharp cliff inherits its full height change | Bake chutes from the first visible ground along the camera ray, check visibility along their downhill route, and constrain each settled fan to a small local slope patch. Found by the first Painted Ages build's deposition and fan fixes (2026-10-12); the bake and air modules that did it were removed in the theme's rebuild later that day, so there is no file to read. |
| #258 | An effect drawn only inside a box test (a prominence, a splash, a decal: `If(abs(s) < w and z < h)`) shows straight-edged rectangles once it grows: an eruption that sways and spreads past its held shape is cut into chunks, with no error | The box was sized for the effect at rest, and its edges became the effect's edges | Grow the box with the effect (by the same share that spreads it) and fade the light out before the box's edge (`1 - smoothstep(0.72 * box, box, abs(s))`), so the box never draws a line. Found on Solar Eclipse's eruptions (2026-10-21), `boxS` / `inBox` in `src/themes/solar-eclipse/solar-eclipse-sun.js` |
| #157 | Many overlapping event pools in a uniform table (a colour held per colony, a glow per landing) wash the scene white where several meet; dimming each one makes a single one unreadable | The pools were summed: seven piece colours over one garden add up to white, and their brightness to seven | Give the hue to the strongest pool and cap the brightness: accumulate `rgb * w³` and `w³` for the hue and `Σw` for the level, return `hue / Σw³ * (Σw / (Σw + 1)) * 2` (one pool reads at 1, ten at 1.8). Precedent: `ocStains` in `src/themes/ocean/ocean-tsl.js` (2026-10-11) |
| #120 | An additive particle system looks right at one count and is a white slab (or a dim speckle) at another tier, in a formation, or on a phone | Exposure was set per particle, so total light scales with the count: five times the particles is five times the light, and additive blending has no ceiling. The same motes packed into a quarter of the area (a formation, a portrait frame) are four times as bright per pixel | Fix the TOTAL light per tier: count × area × exposure is the budget, size falls as the count rises (finer grain), and exposure is derived from the other two; scale exposure down again by how much a layout crowds the motes. Precedent: `swarmLook()` and `applyCrowding()` in `src/themes/murmuration/composition/swarm-tiers.js` |
| #121 | A ring of light or shockwave sent through a thick particle volume reads as a general brightening, not a ring | The wave was a spherical shell in world space. A thick disc seen face on crosses it at a different screen radius at every depth, and a band a unit wide lights a fifth of a nine-unit-tall swarm at once; light that decays slowly behind the front widens the band further | Scale depth down in the wave's distance (`z * 0.3`) so it is a ring ON SCREEN, make the lit band a hairline (0.15–0.4 units), and let the light die within two frames (fade the old light first, then add the new, so the frame the front arrives shows it at full). Precedent: `WAVE_DEPTH` and `FLASH_DECAY` in `src/themes/murmuration/sim/fluid-particles.js` |
| #264 | Dust, pollen or seeds drifting in the air come out as a snowfall: every speck at once, white on the bright haze | The motes were lit by the light of the air round them (the haze's colour, a forward-scatter glow), which reaches every mote alike, so all of them stand over the haze | Light a mote by the DIRECT light at its place only, read from the same occluder the surfaces use (the canopy gobo that draws the sunflecks, `rbFleck(u, P, { lod: 'fixed' })` in the vertex stage), plus a trace of the sky's: most motes are then lost in the shade and a few glint in the sun's patches, exactly where the flecks land on the bark, a light in the air without a shaft. Keep them seek-safe with world-fixed places wrapped round the eye (`fract` over a band a little wider than the lens at each mote's depth, faded over the band's edges), so the camera's breath still gives parallax. Precedent: `createMotes` in `src/themes/rainbark/rainbark-fx.js` (2026-10-21) |
| #220 | Need ballistic event particles (bombs, a fountain's spatter) to read as a burst from kilometres away | — | Under real gravity a 600 m arc seen from 15 km takes twenty seconds and crosses a pixel or two a second: fly them under a time-lapsed gravity (Pyrestorm: 300 m/s² for bombs, 52 for the fountain) and launch each from an apex height (`vy = √(2·g·apex)`, flight = rise + fall to the landing point), and draw a bomb's whole recent arc as a strip whose rows are its closed-form position at earlier times (`clamp(age − (1 − s)·trail, 0, flight)`), the ribbon's sides oriented by the chord so it never twists. The landing point is known when it is thrown, so spawn its cooling ember there for `born + flight`. Pyrestorm's bursts (2026-10-21), `Bombs` in `pyrestorm-fire.js`, `throwBomb` in `pyrestorm-world.js` |
| #126 | A lightning bolt, arc or streamer built in the vertex stage tangles into polygons and loops at one end, while bolts that stand upright in the distance look right | Its sideways wander was measured in METRES (a share of the bolt's length) along an axis crossed with a fixed direction. A bolt that runs INTO the picture (from near the lens to a far tower) is hundreds of metres long and a few degrees wide on screen: metres of wander at its near end are half the frame, and the fixed cross product degenerates when the axis lines up with it | Wander ACROSS the view and measure it as an angle: `aside = normalize(cross(axis, toPoint))` at each point, offset = noise · reach · (the angle the whole bolt spans at the lens) · (that point's distance from the lens). Give limbs their length and their forward direction the same way (the axis with its component along the view ray removed). Found on Voltage Storm's arcs (2026-10-09), `frameAt` in `src/themes/voltage-storm/voltage-storm-bolts.js` |
| #214 | A lightning bolt (a jagged ribbon facing the eye, its side vector taken from each vertex's screen tangent) draws as scattered dashes, with no error | Where the jagged path turns back on screen the tangent flips, the ribbon's two edges swap, and the quads between face away: `FrontSide` culls every other piece. A path that also wanders in depth flips more often | `material.side = THREE.DoubleSide`, and orient each vertex's side vector with the stroke's overall screen direction (`dot(across, perp(B − A)) < 0 ? −across : across`) so the ribbon never twists. Motion-aligned streak quads need `DoubleSide` too (their facing follows the motion). Found on Pyrestorm's bolts (2026-10-13) |
