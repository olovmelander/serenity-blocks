# Serenity Blocks

*Stack · Breathe · Ascend*

Serenity Blocks is a falling-block puzzle game set inside living worlds. A river in spring flood,
a pleasure pier at dusk, a field of ripe barley under a storm sky, a valley under three suns, the
edge of a black hole: more than sixty places, each drawn in real-time 3D, each with its own light
and its own colours for the pieces, over a calm, atmospheric soundtrack.

The world listens while you play. Lock a piece and it answers. Clear a row and light runs through
it. Build a long chain and the whole place rises with you, then settles when you do.

## How it plays

Pieces fall into the well, and you guide each one: move it, turn it, drop it. Fill a row from wall
to wall and it clears. Whatever rested on it falls, and where it lands it can complete new rows,
one wave after another. That is a cascade, and setting one up is the heart of the game.

The board names what you did in its own words: a Quad for four lines at once, a Twist for a piece
turned into a tight slot, a Combo when clear follows clear, a Cascade for the long runs, and a
Clean canvas when the well stands empty.

## Ways to play

- **Single Player.** Every level, a little faster. Keep the stack low and chase your own best.
- **Infinity.** A well one thousand rows tall. Build upward, leave few holes, and chain cascades as
  far as one run will carry you.
- **Odyssey.** A journey from the molten core of the Earth to the edge of a black hole, and beyond.
  Every level brings its own goal and its own world.
- **Stillfall.** Cascade puzzles: a structure, a few pieces and one goal. Gravity waits while you
  choose, then the cascade runs.
- **Local Versus.** Up to four players on one screen, with keyboards, controllers or bots. Clear
  rows to send garbage across, play alone or in teams, and set the rules yourself.
- **Online Versus.** Free-for-all matches for two to eight players, through Steam.

## A place to breathe

- **Serenity.** Nothing to play. A world and its music fill the screen, and a breathing guide is
  there whenever you want it.
- **Sukha Sessions.** Nine voice-guided sessions in worlds made for them: guided breathing to
  unwind, opened one by one as you play and practise.

## Made to be comfortable

- Play with a keyboard or a controller; the menus work with both.
- Reduce flashing effects and Reduce motion are in the settings, and a photosensitivity notice
  opens every launch.
- The worlds come in several quality levels.
