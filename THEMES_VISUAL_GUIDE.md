# Themes Visual Guide

A comprehensive visual description of all available themes in Serenity Blocks. Each theme creates a unique atmospheric backdrop for your Tetris experience.

---

## 🌍 Biomes

### Forest
A serene forest scene with gentle green ambiance. Firefly particles drift through the air, creating a magical woodland atmosphere with soft, natural lighting.

### Himalayan Peak
Majestic mountain peaks reaching toward the sky, creating a sense of grandeur and elevation. The crisp, high-altitude atmosphere brings a feeling of tranquility and vastness.

### Ice Temple
A mystical frozen sanctuary with crystalline structures and ethereal ice formations. The scene glows with cool blue and white tones, evoking a sense of ancient, frozen beauty.

### Moonlit Forest
A forest bathed in soft moonlight, where shadows dance and the world takes on a mystical, silver-blue hue. The moon casts gentle light through the trees, creating a peaceful nocturnal atmosphere.

### Wolfhour
A mysterious twilight hour when the world is caught between day and night. The atmosphere is moody and atmospheric, perfect for contemplative gameplay.

### Ocean
An underwater scene with kelp strands swaying in the current and god rays filtering down from the surface. The aquatic environment creates a sense of depth and movement, with marine life and water effects.

### Sunset
A warm, golden sunset scene with rich oranges, pinks, and purples painting the sky. The horizon glows with the last light of day, creating a peaceful, romantic atmosphere.

### Mountain
Towering peaks and valleys create a sense of scale and majesty. The mountain landscape offers dramatic vistas and natural beauty.

### Winter
A snowy winter wonderland with gently falling snowflakes. The scene captures the quiet beauty of winter, with soft white landscapes and a crisp, cold atmosphere.

### Fall
Autumn colors dominate the scene with rich reds, oranges, and yellows. Falling leaves drift through the air, capturing the essence of the changing seasons.

### Summer
Bright, warm summer days with vibrant colors and sunny skies. The atmosphere is cheerful and energetic, evoking long summer afternoons.

### Spring
Fresh spring scenery with blooming flowers and new growth. The palette is bright and hopeful, with greens and pastel colors celebrating renewal and life.

### Sakura Twilight
A beautiful sunset scene with cherry blossom trees. Pink petals fall continuously through the air, while glowing paper lanterns and flying bird silhouettes add to the magical twilight atmosphere. The scene combines the beauty of cherry blossoms with the warm glow of sunset.

### Koi Pond
A serene top-down view of a koi pond. Gracefully swimming koi fish move through the water, while lily pads bob gently on the surface. Water ripples follow the fish, and cherry blossom petals float on the shimmering water surface with light reflections.

### Meadow
An idyllic sun-dappled meadow with a warm golden sun featuring animated rays and lens flare. Drifting clouds create parallax depth, while swaying grass responds to wind gusts. Colorful wildflowers bloom and sway, butterflies dance with varied flight paths, and floating pollen and dandelion seeds drift through the air. Magical fireflies glow at dusk.

### Swedish Forest
A Nordic forest scene with the characteristic beauty of Swedish woodlands. The atmosphere captures the essence of Scandinavian nature.

### Desert Oasis
A desert landscape with an oasis providing a cool, refreshing contrast. The scene balances the heat of the desert with the life-giving presence of water.

### Misty Lake
A tranquil lake scene shrouded in mist. The atmosphere is calm and mysterious, with soft fog creating depth and atmosphere.

### Waves
Ocean waves crash and flow, creating a dynamic, rhythmic scene. The movement of water is the central focus, bringing energy and motion to the background.

### Luminous Tides
Glowing, bioluminescent tides wash ashore, creating an otherworldly beach scene. The water itself seems to glow with inner light, creating a magical coastal atmosphere.

### Stillwater
A calm, still body of water reflecting the sky above. The scene is peaceful and meditative, with perfect reflections creating symmetry and tranquility.

### Moonlit Greenhouse
A greenhouse bathed in moonlight, where plants grow under the silver glow. The scene combines the beauty of nature with the structure of glass and metal, creating a unique indoor-outdoor atmosphere.

### Moonrise Summit
A mountain peak at the moment of moonrise. The scene captures the transition from day to night, with the moon ascending over the summit.

## 🌌 Cosmic & Sky

### Aurora
Dynamic northern lights that grow, shrink, fade, and intensify organically over time. The aurora can appear as a subtle horizon glow, a medium curtain, or a massive full-screen storm. Deep green and emerald colors dominate, creating a living sky that constantly evolves.

### Galaxy
An immersive cosmic experience with multi-layered nebula clouds that have depth and rotation. A dynamic starfield with varying intensities, cosmic dust particles drifting through space, and distant spiral galaxies create a sense of vast cosmic scale. Supernova bursts and cosmic ripple waves react to gameplay.

### Geode
A luminous fiber-optic starfield creating an immersive cosmic cavern. Dense stars span the entire screen, with fiber-optic strands hanging and floating throughout. Dynamic sparkle bursts react to gameplay events. The warm cosmic color palette features oranges, reds, and magentas, creating a cozy yet otherworldly atmosphere.

### Aether Tides
Ethereal cosmic tides flow through space, creating waves of energy and light. The scene has a mystical, flowing quality that suggests the movement of cosmic forces.

### Cosmic Chimes
A celestial scene where cosmic forces create harmonious, chiming effects. The atmosphere is both cosmic and musical, with visual elements that suggest sound and vibration.

### Starlight
A star-filled sky where individual stars twinkle and shine. The scene captures the beauty of a clear night sky, with stars of varying brightness creating depth.

### Bioluminescence
An underwater scene filled with glowing bioluminescent plants and crystal formations. Various glowing organisms create their own light, making the scene feel like a deep-sea wonderland where life itself provides illumination.

### Crystal Cave
A cave filled with glowing crystals that create their own light. The scene combines the mystery of underground spaces with the beauty of crystalline formations.

### Lunara
A breathtaking mystical winter landscape featuring a twinkling starfield with colored stars. Dynamic aurora borealis with multiple layers, twin planets (purple and pink) with pulsing glow, layered mountain silhouettes with atmospheric glow, snow-covered pine trees with gentle sway, falling snowflakes, and mystical fog/mist layers.

### Solar Eclipse
The dramatic moment of a solar eclipse, where the moon passes before the sun. The scene captures the unique lighting and atmosphere of this celestial event.

### Black Hole
A gravitational anomaly in space, with stars and light being pulled toward a central point. The scene creates a sense of cosmic power and the distortion of space-time itself.

### Supernova
A star exploding in a brilliant burst of light and energy. The scene captures the moment of stellar death and rebirth, with expanding waves of light and color.

### Cosmic Noir
A dark, moody cosmic scene with film noir aesthetics. The palette is darker, creating a mysterious space atmosphere with dramatic lighting.

### Blood Moon
A lunar eclipse where the moon takes on a deep red hue. The scene is dramatic and atmospheric, with the red moon casting an eerie glow.

### Astral Weave
Cosmic threads and energies weave together, creating patterns of light and energy. The scene suggests the interconnected nature of the universe.

### Stellar Velocity
Stars streak through space at high velocity, creating motion blur and a sense of incredible speed. The scene captures the feeling of traveling through space at light speed.

## 🎨 Abstract & Artistic

### Fluid Dreams
A flowing, dreamlike scene with organic, fluid-like movements. The atmosphere is abstract and surreal, suggesting the fluid nature of dreams and imagination.

### Nebula Flow
A GPU-accelerated fluid dynamics simulation creating organic, flowing visual effects. The fluid moves autonomously, creating swirling patterns and waves. Multiple color schemes (cosmic, ocean, aurora, fire, prismatic) allow for different moods. The fluid reacts spectacularly to gameplay events with spirals, waves, and bursts.

### Electric Dreams
An electric, neon-drenched scene with vibrant colors and energy. The atmosphere is futuristic and energetic, with electric currents and neon glows.

### Chromatic Impasto
An artistic scene inspired by thick, textured paint application. Colors are rich and layered, creating depth through visual texture and impasto techniques.

### Voltage Storm
A high-energy scene with electric visuals and neon colors. Lightning and shockwave effects create a sense of electrical power and intensity. The fluid simulation moves at high velocity with electric/neon aesthetics.

### Chromadelic Highway
A psychedelic highway scene with vibrant, shifting colors. The scene suggests movement and travel through a colorful, abstract landscape.

---

## 🏙️ Urban & Modern

### Rainy Window
A window during a rainstorm, with raindrops running down the glass. Ripples and splashes create realistic water effects, while occasional lightning illuminates the scene. Mist particles add atmosphere, creating a cozy, indoor feeling while watching the storm outside.

### Neon Dusk
An urban scene at dusk, with neon lights beginning to glow. The atmosphere captures the transition from day to night in a city, with electric colors starting to dominate.

### Synthwave Sunset
A retro-futuristic sunset scene with a grid pattern overlay. The sun moves with animated rays, and the scene has a distinctive 1980s synthwave aesthetic with vibrant pinks, purples, and oranges.

### Cinder Drift
Embers and cinders drift through the air, creating a warm, glowing atmosphere. The scene suggests fire and warmth, with particles of light floating like embers.
