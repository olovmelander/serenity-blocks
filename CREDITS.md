## Voice

The breathing guide's narration was made by Olov Melander with ElevenLabs (AI text to speech).

## Sound

The sound effects are synthesised in code by the game itself. The two launch sounds (`begin`
and `warp`) were generated with Stable Audio (Stability AI).

## Images and models made with AI tools

- **Neon Quarter's shop paintings and advert pictures** (in the `shopfronts` and `billboards`
  atlases under `textures/neon-district/`): generated by Olov Melander with Google Gemini. The
  district's own brand, Hale and travel posters in the billboard atlas are drawn by the game's
  build script, and the travel posters frame the game's own pictures of its worlds.
- **Nebula pictures** (the nebula textures in `textures/blood-moon/` and `textures/wolfhour/`,
  and `textures/rainbow-nebula.png`): generated by Olov Melander with Google Gemini. One of them
  is based on a Hubble image, credited below.
- **3D models made with the TRELLIS.2 image-to-3D model** and finished for the game: the manta
  and the whale of Odyssey chapter 2, the goldfinch and the swallow of chapter 3, the snow
  conifers on Odyssey's mountains, and Winter's arctic fox (rigged and painted by the game's
  own script).

## Made for this game

Built by this project's own scripts in Blender or in code, with no image or 3D generative model
and no third-party source:

- **Models:** Crystal Cave's cavern; Fall's maple, oak and birch trees and its red squirrel;
  Forest's trees; Golden Forest's trees, rowboat and jetty; Grainfields' groves, horses, geese and
  ruin; Himalayan Peak's golden eagle (also flying in Odyssey chapter 4); Ocean's sea creatures
  (also visiting Odyssey chapter 2); Sakura Twilight's cherry trees, lanterns, pagoda and
  mountain; Spring's trees and rocks; Stillwater's troll and spirit; Summer's trees, cottage,
  boathouse and maypole; Sunfall's ledge, shell and crowns; Synthwave Sunset's gull; Verdant
  Hills' oak, field trees, windmill and sheep; Winter's polar bear.
- **Neon Quarter's people:** twenty-four characters, modelled, animated and rendered to sprite
  sheets in Blender.
- **The logo and the app icon**, drawn in code; the menu pictures are renders of the game.
- Everything else on screen (skies, water, land, weather, light, particles and most creatures)
  is generated by the game's code as it runs.
