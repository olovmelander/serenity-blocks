# Crystal Cave assets — provenance

Everything in this directory is generated by `scripts/blender/crystal_cave_assets.py`
(helpers in `scripts/blender/crystal_cave/`). Nothing is downloaded, scanned or traced:
every vertex comes from the generator's own height fields, noise and growth rules, so the
pack is project-owned. No third-party model, texture or HDRI is involved.

- **Tool:** Blender 4.5.9 LTS, headless (`--background --factory-startup`), Cycles on CPU
  for the light bakes. GLB written by the repo's own quantising writer
  (`scripts/blender/fall_grove/glb.py`).
- **License:** project-owned, ships with the game under the game's own terms.

## Regenerate

```
"C:\Program Files\Blender Foundation\Blender 4.5\blender.exe" --background --factory-startup ^
    --python scripts/blender/crystal_cave_assets.py -- --out src/themes/crystal-cave/assets
```

Add `--draft` for a two-minute pass (coarser rock, fewer bake samples) while composing,
and `--preview <dir>` for two Cycles stills of the lit hall. A full run takes about a
quarter of an hour, nearly all of it the seven light bakes. The run is deterministic
except for Monte-Carlo noise in the baked light, so `asset-manifest.json` (bytes and
SHA-256, pinned by `tests/unit/crystal-cave-assets.test.js`) changes with every run:
commit the GLB and the manifest together.

The composition is authored in `scripts/blender/crystal_cave/layout.py` (where clusters
grow and where play may sprout new ones) and `fields.py` (the shape of the hall, its
columns, islands, stalactites and skylight).

## `cavern.glb`

One file, four nodes. Positions are in game units, y up, the player looking down −z.

| Node | Mode | Contents |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `cavern` | triangles | The hall. `POSITION` (int16, restored by the node's scale and translation), `NORMAL` (int8), and the baked light: `_LIGHT0` = aqua, amethyst, sapphire, rose; `_LIGHT1` = amber, pool, skylight, ambient occlusion (uint8, stored as `sqrt(light / lightScale[channel])`); `_LDIR` (int8), the dominant direction that light arrives from. |
| `crystals` | points | One record per crystal: `POSITION` (root), `_QUAT`, `_DIMS` (radius, depth, height, tip), `_LOOK` (apex x, apex z, family, glow), `_META` (seed, group, rank, 0). `group` 0 is always present; `group` n > 0 belongs to sprout site n − 1 and only appears once play has grown it. |
| `glowworms` | points | `POSITION`, `_PARAMS` (size, phase, hue, thread). |
| `drips` | points | Stalactite tips over open water: `POSITION`, `_PARAMS` (stalactite length, 0, 0, 0). |

Scene `extras` carry the schema version, pool level, family and channel names,
`lightScale`, the skylight (position, target, cone angle), the camera the picture is
composed for, the sprout sites and the element counts.

### How the light is baked

The crystals of one mineral family are the only lamps in the scene (white, weighted by
each crystal's glow); Cycles bakes the diffuse light, direct and bounced, that reaches
every cavern vertex. That is repeated per family, then for the pool surface, then for a
spot lamp shining down the skylight's chimney, then for ambient occlusion. The eight
scalars let the runtime re-colour and pulse each source independently — a family that
flares during play flares on the stone around it, with the bake's soft shadows.
