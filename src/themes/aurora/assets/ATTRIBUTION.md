# Aurora conifers

`winter-conifers.glb` is original Serenity Blocks artwork. The Blender generator builds a Norway spruce and a Scots pine, each at two levels of detail. Branches, needle fans, snow caps and contact snow are modeled geometry. Vertex colors identify bark, needles, snow and exposure to the sky. The meshes also carry attachment points for falling powder.

The source is [aurora_conifers.py](../../../../scripts/blender/aurora_conifers.py). From the repository root, regenerate with `blender --background --python scripts/blender/aurora_conifers.py`.
