# Cinder Drift basalt fan

`basalt-fan.json` is an original Serenity Blocks model, generated in Blender 5.3 and distributed under the game's terms. Its sloping column joints and fallen drums are cut from seeded polygon rings. The two shores instance the same indexed Three.js geometry.

From the repository root, regenerate the asset with `blender --background --factory-startup --python scripts/blender/cinder_drift_basalt.py`.
