# Lunara asset attribution

Lunara’s moon atlas is an adaptation of the Moon and Mars maps by **Solar System Scope**, used under Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International. The adaptation is available under the same licence. [Source textures](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/) · [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/)

The Blender bake reads `public/textures/2k_moon.jpg` and `public/textures/2k_mars.jpg`. It grades their luminance separately, adds overlapping lunar impact basins, terraces and ejecta, and calculates two surface-slope channels. The resulting `public/textures/lunara/twin-moons.png` holds the great moon’s albedo and relief in RGB and the red companion’s terrain in alpha. The shaders supply the lighting, colours and emissive veins. [Blender source](../../../../scripts/blender/lunara_moons.py) · [Game credits](../../../../CREDITS.md)

The crystal spires, mountain ranges, shoreline, flora, lake, sky and gameplay effects are generated by Lunara’s scene code. The picker icon is a render of that scene and includes the adapted moon map.
