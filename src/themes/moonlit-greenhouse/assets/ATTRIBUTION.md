# Moonlit Greenhouse assets

The Phalaenopsis orchid spray is original Blender geometry made for Serenity Blocks.
The lunar disc is a cropped orthographic projection with adjusted contrast of the bundled
`public/textures/2k_moon.jpg` by Solar System Scope. Both are baked by
`scripts/blender/moonlit_greenhouse.py`. The lunar map is licensed under
[CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/). [Solar System Scope textures](https://www.solarsystemscope.com/textures/)
