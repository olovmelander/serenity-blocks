# Odyssey chapter 4 asset attribution

## Golden eagle (`eagle.glb`)

This original model is the October 2026 edition of Himalayan Peak's Blender eagle. Odyssey
keeps its own 2,300-triangle, 26-bone copy with the plumage in vertex colours. The generator
for this edition is `scripts/blender/himalayan_eagle.py` at revision `4da127b2c`.

Odyssey poses it in code (`src/rendering/odyssey/composition/odyssey-eagle-flight.js`): it
thermals off the hero massif's south flank over chapters 4-5.
