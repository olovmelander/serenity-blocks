# Chapter 3 Flying Bird Assets

Skinned, vertex-coloured songbirds that fly the Odyssey Chapter 3 Surface World
corridor. These replace the previous static Quaternius `bird-jay` flights.

- `goldfinch-flying.glb` — Goldfinch (skinned, "Flap" animation, vertex-coloured)
- `swallow-flying.glb` — Swallow (skinned, "Flap" animation, vertex-coloured)

Provenance: original assets produced in the project owner's local photo→3D→rig
asset pipeline (the `C:\AI` ComfyUI + Blender toolchain), exported via the
Khronos glTF Blender I/O exporter. They are project-owned originals, not
third-party/CC0 assets, so they are intentionally kept out of the Quaternius
CC0 bundle (`../quaternius/`) and retain their baked vertex colours.
