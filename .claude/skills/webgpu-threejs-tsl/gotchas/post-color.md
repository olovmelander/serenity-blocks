# Post chain, tone and colour

Read before building or changing a theme's `*-post.js`, its bloom, tone curve or grade, or any screen-space effect; and when colours come out pastel, milky, veiled or hue-shifted, or a post pass draws in the wrong place.

**House chain.** One scene pass (`sceneSamples()` for MSAA), one bloom, ONE output pass; tone mapping happens exactly once, in that pass (`renderer.toneMapping = NoToneMapping`, `pipeline.outputColorTransform = false`). Judge colour only through the finished chain, and look at a false-colour view of the pre-tone-map values before tuning bloom.

One gotcha per line; the number is the row's permanent id (cited elsewhere as "gotcha #NN"). Rows numbered below 300 were numbered before these files existed and keep their numbers, in no order. A NEW row goes at the END of this table and takes the next free number in **900–999**. To search: `node .claude/skills/webgpu-threejs-tsl/scripts/gotcha.mjs <words>` (`README.md` in this folder).

| #243 | A dark scene goes milky (every black lifted to a dim veil of the light's colour) once light in the air is added, though false colour shows the glow is local and the stone alone, with the air switched off, keeps its blacks | Two causes, both tiny in linear light and plain after sRGB (a linear 0.005 is about 0.07 on screen).
