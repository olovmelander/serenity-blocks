# Light and shadow: shadow maps, shafts and analytic shadows

Read before adding a shadow map, a `GodraysNode`, light shafts or any shadow you compute yourself; and when shadows grain, the sky veils over, or shafts read as a flat haze.

**House default.** Themes are self-shaded (`MeshBasicNodeMaterial`, no scene lights): most shadows and shafts are analytic terms in the shader. A real shadow map is the exception, used through `shadow(light)` with a fixed, noise-free filter and, for a fixed light, `shadow.autoUpdate = false`.

One gotcha per line; the number is the row's permanent id (cited elsewhere as "gotcha #NN"). Rows numbered below 300 were numbered before these files existed and keep their numbers, in no order. A NEW row goes at the END of this table and takes the next free number in **800–899**. To search: `node .claude/skills/webgpu-threejs-tsl/scripts/gotcha.mjs <words>` (`README.md` in this folder).

| #84 | Need real sun shadows in an unlit (`MeshBasicNodeMaterial`) scene | — | `const lit = float(shadow(light))` from `three/tsl` works standalone: share ONE node across all materials, add the light to the scene, set `renderer.shadowMap.enabled = true` and `castShadow` on casters. For a fixed light set `light.shadow.autoUpdate = false` and raise `needsUpdate` over the first frames (async pipelines can still be missing from the first map). Verified on both backends. Precedent: `src/themes/fall/fall-light.js` |
| #83 | `[Texture "ShadowDepthTexture"] usage (TextureBinding\|RenderAttachment) ... same synchronization scope`, then invalid command buffers | A shadow **caster** samples the shadow map in its `colorNode` (unlit materials that call `shadow(light)` themselves). The shadow pass builds each caster's alpha as `material.colorNode.a` (`Renderer._getShadowNodes`), so the whole colour graph — including the shadow lookup — runs while the map is the render target | Put a caster's shading in `material.fragmentNode = vec4(colour, 1)` and leave `colorNode` unset. Non-casters may keep `colorNode`. Precedent: `src/themes/fall/fall-forest.js` |
| #201 | A fine diagonal crosshatch (a screen-door grain) over lit surfaces, foliage and bark, on both backends and on tiers without post; worst under a LOW sun | three r186's `PCFShadowFilter` takes five compares on a Vogel disc turned per pixel by interleaved gradient noise (`ShadowFilterNode.js`), which expects a temporal filter to average it. Without TAA the noise stays, and under a grazing sun nearly every surface sits at the edge of its own shadow, so the whole frame shows it. MSAA does not help (shading is per pixel) | Give the light a fixed filter: `light.shadow.filterNode = Fn(({ depthTexture, shadowCoord }) => …)` summing `texture(depthTexture, uv + offset).compare(shadowCoord.z)` over a fixed 3 × 3 grid (each tap is already a smooth 2 × 2 hardware PCF, since PCF shadow maps get `LinearFilter`), four taps on low tiers. Same penumbra width, no grain. Found on Fall's second pass (2026-10-20); `gridShadowFilter` in `src/themes/shared/grid-shadow-filter.js`, used by Fall and Golden Forest |
| #85 | `GodraysNode` throws / reads `depthTexture` of null at build | It reads `light.shadow.map` inside `setup()`, and that map only exists after some material that uses the light's shadow has been built | Render the scene once (`renderer.render(scene, camera)`) before the first `pipeline.render()`; then `godrays(scenePassDepth, camera, light)`. Works on WebGPU and the WebGL2 backend. Precedent: `src/themes/fall/fall-post.js` |
| #86 | `THREE.TSL: "rtt()" does not allow overwriting the value` every frame | `bilateralBlur(node)` was given a non-texture node (e.g. the `GodraysNode` itself); it ping-pongs its input texture and cannot swap an `rtt()` wrapper | Pass the pass texture: `bilateralBlur(godraysNode.getTextureNode(), ...)` |
| #202 | The sky behind a forest or a ridge is a milky, pale lavender veil on the tiers that have volumetric shafts, but a clear blue on the tiers without them | `GodraysNode` marches the shadow map along each view ray up to the scene depth; toward the sky dome that is hundreds of metres of lit air, so the march saturates (`maxDensity`) and the shaft colour is added over the whole sky | Fade the shafts' contribution by distance where the view reaches the dome (`perspectiveDepthToViewZ(sceneDepth, near, far)` with the camera's near/far as uniforms; beyond the last scenery keep only a glow round the sun) and let the dome carry its own air. Found on Fall's second pass (2026-10-20), `skyward` in `src/themes/fall/fall-post.js` |
| #247 | Need cast shadows on a procedural heightfield (a cloud deck, dunes, snow) under a low sun, without a shadow map | — | Tap along the sun's azimuth: the ray toward the sun climbs `tan(elevation)` per unit, and a tap where the field stands above it is shadow, `smoothstep(-pen, pen, h0 + t·tan − h(p + t·ŝ))` with `pen` growing with `t`. Carry the slow octaves to each tap along their analytic gradients and re-read only the fast ones; leave the smallest octave out of the farthest tap. Tall props on the same ground shadow it analytically: where the ray crosses a prop's plane, compare its height and lateral offset with the prop's width profile (long lanes for a few operations a prop). Precedent: `src/themes/nimbus-veil/nimbus-veil-cloudsea.js` (2026-10-21) |
| #232 | Need coloured or crisp shadow shafts in a low mist (the shadows of tall props standing in the air) without a ray march, on both backends | — | Write each caster as a flat-topped vertical cylinder; under a directional light its shadow is a wedge bounded by four planes (the strip it sweeps away from the light, its far side, the slanted plane through its top edge), so the view ray's span through it is the intersection of four half-lines `p + q·t < 0` (a few lines, a laid-out pure `Fn`). An exponential height mist integrates in closed form over any span, `σ₀e^{−h₀/H}(e^{−k a} − e^{−k b})/k` with `k = D.y/H`, so each light's in-scatter is the whole ray's depth minus its shadow spans (clamped at 0). Per light, per caster, no march, no shadow map; same casters as the surfaces' shadows, so the wedges stand over their stripes. Precedent: `sbShadowSpan` / `mistLight` in `src/themes/sunbraid/sunbraid-tsl.js`, run in the post at reduced resolution (2026-10-20) |
| #166 | Volumetric light shafts under a glass roof (a glasshouse, a conservatory, any mostly clear canopy) read as a uniform milky veil with a few faint dark lines, however the march, the phase function or the density is tuned | Shafts are lit air against UNLIT air, and clear glazing passes about 85 % of the light everywhere: only the bars and ribs shadow it, so nearly all the air is lit and the beams have nothing to stand out from | Put large occluders into the light function itself, not into the mist: lath blinds rolled part-way down a hashed share of the roof's bays (`BLINDS`, `mgBlind`), and the palms' crowns as an analytic canopy at their known positions (`mgCanopy`: where the ray toward the moon crosses each crown's height, a fan of narrowing fronds made of leaflets), picked each time the light moves by where their shadows actually land. The beams then alternate bay by bay and are ruled by laths and fronds. Keep the hero light itself clear: lift the blind on the bay the eye sees the moon through (`U.moonBay`). Found on Moonlit Greenhouse (2026-10-11), `src/themes/moonlit-greenhouse/moonlit-greenhouse-tsl.js` |
| #242 | Need shafts of light (god rays) from a point light inside a ray-marched scene without marching the air, sharp at any width and free of sampling noise | — | Give the light an emission pattern (a gem's facets: N narrow cones, `exp(k (\|ω · a\| − 1))` per axis `a`) and integrate each cone along the view ray in closed form: seen from the light, the ray's points sweep ONE arc of directions, `ω(θ) = cos θ · ê + sin θ · d` with `θ = atan((t − tc) / h)` (h the closest approach, ê toward it), and the in-scatter per unit θ is the light's over h, so with `A = ê · a`, `B = d · a`, `R = √(A² + B²)`, `φ = atan2(B, A)` each end of a beam is a Gaussian in θ about φ (and φ ± π): `exp(k (R − 1)) · √(π / 2kR) · (erf((θb − φ)√(kR/2)) − erf((θa − φ)√(kR/2)))`. Skip the axes with `R < 1 − ln(100)/k` before any erf (most of them, for most rays), integrate BOTH ends (picking the one nearer the range's middle draws a straight seam where the pick flips), and use a polynomial erf (Abramowitz & Stegun 7.1.27: no exp, no sqrt). Shadow each beam with ONE lookup where it crosses the ray (θ = its peak, clamped to the range): an octahedral map of the distance to the first stone in each direction from the light, marched only when the scene changes. Loop over the axes from a `uniformArray` (six beams unrolled at two sites were 15 KB of WGSL). Kaleidosonic's light pass (2026-10-21): `ksBeamLobe` and the light map in `src/themes/kaleidosonic/kaleidosonic-light.js`, `beamsAlong` in `kaleidosonic-temple.js` |
| #252 | A ray-marched light shaft (a beam through a skylight, a spotlight with a gobo) is covered in a static screen-door grain, a fine crosshatch over its whole body, though its density noise is smooth; more steps only shrink it | The shaft's mask is a 2D texture projected along the light (a vine fringe, leaves, a window's bars), and each march step reads it at ONE point. A view ray through the shaft is a straight line in that texture, and a step 5–20 m long skips over every streak narrower than that: each pixel's jittered steps hit or miss them differently. Mip selection by distance (the sun disc's penumbra) does not help: it blurs across the streaks, never along the step | Read the mask with explicit gradients: the major axis is the segment of the texture one step covers (the ray's direction projected along the light, times the step), the minor axis the penumbra's width across it, and the texture's `anisotropy = 16`, so the hardware averages the mask along the step and keeps the streaks sharp across it (`texture(t, uv).grad(gx, gy)`; for a polar map convert both vectors with its Jacobian, `d·e_t / (2πr)` and `d·e_r / reach`). Integrate any thin layer the shaft crosses (a cloud deck 13 m thick) in closed form (erf) apart from the march, and drop noise octaves finer than the step. Found on Sunfall's column (2026-10-21): `sfMaskAt`'s `dq` in `src/themes/sunfall/sunfall-tsl.js`, `deckBand` in `sunfall-post.js` |
