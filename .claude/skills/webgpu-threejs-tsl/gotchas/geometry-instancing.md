# Geometry, instancing and vertex data

Read before writing a `positionNode` or `vertexNode`, instancing anything, merging or generating a mesh in code, or loading a glTF; and when instances collapse, a mesh never draws on WebGPU, or a generated mesh shades flat or dull.

**House pattern for many small things.** `InstancedBufferGeometry` with ONE `InstancedInterleavedBuffer` per pool (WebGPU binds at most eight vertex buffers), integer-coded per-instance data, closed-form motion in the vertex stage, every pool always drawn with dormant slots at zero size.

One gotcha per line; the number is the row's permanent id (cited elsewhere as "gotcha #NN"). Rows numbered below 300 were numbered before these files existed and keep their numbers, in no order. A NEW row goes at the END of this table and takes the next free number in **700–799**. To search: `node .claude/skills/webgpu-threejs-tsl/scripts/gotcha.mjs <words>` (`README.md` in this folder).

| # | Symptom | Cause | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| #65 | Instanced mask/displacement lands in wrong space, or instancing collapses to one transform | r185 **inverted** `positionNode` ordering (verified from generated WGSL): r181 ran `positionNode` first (`positionLocal` inside it = raw geometry) and applied instance/batch/skin matrices *after* its output; r185 applies the matrices *first* (`positionLocal` is post-instance inside `positionNode`) and the node's output is **final** | Portable idiom: compute masks/pivots/phases from `positionGeometry`, output `positionLocal.add(displacement)`. Never output a `positionGeometry`-only expression on a real-matrix InstancedMesh — on r185 that discards the instance matrices |
| #66 | Instanced/point particles invisible or collapsed to a dot | `positionNode = buffer.element(instanceIndex)` replaces *every vertex* with one point | `positionLocal.add(buffer.element(instanceIndex))`, or the `vertexNode` billboard pattern in `src/themes/winter/winter-snowfall.js` |
| #99 | `Render pipeline creation failed (...): Vertex buffer count (9) exceeds the maximum number of vertex buffers (8)`, the mesh never draws on WebGPU (the WebGL2 backend draws it) | On WebGPU every `BufferAttribute` is its own vertex buffer and a pipeline may bind eight. `position` + `uv` + seven instanced attributes is nine | Pack the per-instance attributes into `InstancedInterleavedBuffer`s (one for what never changes, one `DynamicDrawUsage` for what gameplay writes) and expose them as `InterleavedBufferAttribute`s under the same names; `attribute('aName', 'vec4')` reads them unchanged, and several draws can share the buffers. Found on Lunara's spires (2026-10-05), `lunara-crystals.js` |
| #161 | `BufferGeometryUtils.mergeGeometries` returns `null` ("make sure index attribute exists among all geometries, or in none of them") and the merged mesh is missing | `ExtrudeGeometry` and the `PolyhedronGeometry` family (icosahedron, …) are non-indexed; `BoxGeometry`, `SphereGeometry`, `CylinderGeometry`, `LatheGeometry`, `PlaneGeometry` are indexed. The merge needs an index on all or none, and identical attribute names (Extrude also carries `uv`) | Give each non-indexed piece a trivial index (`0…n−1`, `Uint32Array` above 65 535 vertices) and strip every piece to one attribute set before merging. Same rebuild, `ensureAttrs` in `src/themes/rainbow-terraces/rainbow-terraces-isles.js` |
| #163 | Rocks grown from `IcosahedronGeometry` (displaced by noise) shade faceted however high the detail | The polyhedron family is non-indexed: every face has its own three vertices, so `computeVertexNormals` gives flat normals | Delete `normal` and `uv`, weld with `BufferGeometryUtils.mergeVertices(geo, 1e-5)`, then displace and `computeVertexNormals`. Rainbow Terraces' crags, trees and far isles (`icosphere` in `rainbow-terraces-isles.js`) |
| #118 | A generated mesh is dull, never catches the key light, shows no specular — but nothing is missing and nothing errors (a fish's back darker than its fins, flat dark lily pads) | Its triangles are wound the wrong way, so `computeVertexNormals()` gave normals that point INTO the surface. With `side: DoubleSide` (or an unlit material that reads its own `varying` normal, which three does not flip for back faces) the mesh still draws, lit from behind: `dot(N, L)` is negative on the faces you see | Check the facing numerically when you build a mesh in code — the mean of `normal.y` over a surface that should face up, or `sign(normal.z) === sign(position.z)` on a body's flanks — and pin it in a unit test (`tests/unit/koi-pond-meshes.test.js`). Swap two indices per triangle to fix. Found on Koi Pond's koi and lily pads (2026-10-08): both were lit by ambient light alone until a close-up showed fins brighter than bodies |
| #249 | A generated tube's end piece (a maw, a lip, a cap) shows its INSIDE material from outside (teeth on a closed dome) and its outside where the eye looks in, though the body's inside/outside test (`frontFacing`) is right | The piece's rings were appended running the other way (rim → apex while the body runs head → tail), so its triangles wind the other way and its front faces are its inside | Flip the test on that piece: `inside = select(frontFacing, isCap, 1 - isCap)` with the piece's flag as a per-vertex attribute, and capture the piece closed AND open. Found on Shifting Sands' worm (2026-10-21), `createWorm` in `src/themes/shifting-sands/shifting-sands-worm.js` |
| #97 | `THREE.TSL: 'dFdx' is not supported in the vertex stage` on a loaded glTF | The model ships without normals (the Khronos sample fox does), so `normalWorld` falls back to screen-space derivatives; a `varying(...)` that contains it evaluates them in the vertex stage | Set `material.flatShading = true` after construction (it is not a constructor option of `MeshBasicNodeMaterial`) and keep the normal out of vertex-stage expressions. Precedent: `src/themes/sakura-twilight/sakura-foxes.js` |
| #159 | Tetromino pieces built as four rounded cubes read as "four bits" (grooves and seams between the cells), not as the game's solid pieces; the user asked for them back as solid colour | Each cell was its own rounded box, so every shared face left a groove and a crease in the normals | Build each shape ONCE as a fused solid: trace the polyomino's outline (shared edges cancel), inset it by the bevel size and round its corners, extrude with `ExtrudeGeometry` (`bevelSize = bevelThickness`, depth `1 − 2·bevel`), delete `uv`/`normal`, `mergeVertices`, `computeVertexNormals`. Seven instanced meshes share one material; a locked piece's cells are matched to (shape, quarter turns) and the turn goes in its quaternion. Shade as polished solid colour (own colour lit by a key and a top light, a light of its own, clear-coat Fresnel, a glint); refraction, if any, only as a minority term. Same rebuild, `createShapeGeometry` / `matchShape` in `serenity-warp-glass.js` |
